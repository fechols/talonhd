# S1 models

The S1 roster in HD. The base bible entries (`of`) keep everything about identity, behaviour, hit points
and patterns; this file decides how each one is built as a model, which angles are pre-rendered, and which
role each part's paint becomes.

## Notes per entity

- **WASP**: pure Lattice, so mostly `hot` over `dark`. 16 yaws × 3 banks: its S-curves bank.
- **GANNET**: the dive is a **pitch** toward the viewer. In top-down 3D that looks like the gull wings
  folding as it drops.
- **SKIFF, PILLBOX, HATCH, CRAWLER**: ground and sea units are sprites in HD (there is no bitmap layer),
  drawn over the panorama. Their bases sit one luminance step darker than their moving parts.
- **KEELBREAKER**: an XL destroyer longer than the screen. Its three turrets and the bridge core are
  separate sprites, so they can track and die independently.
- **GANTRY QUEEN**: the stage boss becomes a **flying** boss. After rising from the apron, the
  recrystallised control tower hovers at the top of the screen, sways across it and banks into each turn:
  512×512, three screens wide. The player never sees all of it, which is the point. Her legs and arms are
  separate rigged parts. In phase 2 the stage's `hot` goes from cyan to yellow.
