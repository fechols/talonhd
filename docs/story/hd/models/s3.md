# S3 models

The S3 roster in HD, and the direction for REDLINE's 3D level.

## Notes per entity

- **DUST DEVIL**: a rotor, not a ship. It spins forever, and 8 samples over its 3-fold symmetry make
  that smooth at any speed.
- **RAIL CAR, SIDEWINDER**: trains ride the level's own rails, so the rails are where the danger runs.
  - The flatcar yaws to its rail. The turret is a second sprite that tracks on its own.
  - The SIDEWINDER's cars reuse the RAIL CAR's turret frames.
- **SANDCRAWLER**: it rises out of the level's own sand in 4 poses, after a dust ring that warns of it
  two seconds before.
- **WATER TOWER**: the legs and the long shadow are level geometry. Only the crystal-split lid is a
  sprite, as with S2's ROOF HATCH.
- **VULTURE**: the first big enemy aircraft. It slides instead of turning, and it stays mostly dark, so
  the yellow CARACARA is the brightest ship on screen.
- **LANCER**: a spear that hangs, turns to the ship and drops. Its shiver is a 1-px offset, never a
  blink.
- **WASP, CRAWLER**: S1's models, recolored by the palette.
- **TRESTLE TITAN**: the gantry is the body. Its jibs slew the claws through mirrored arcs, because from
  above that is the arc the bible's sweep draws; a pendulum would only slide in a line.
  - The claws are the targets in phase 1. The boiler door is the target in phase 2.
- **CARACARA** (`ships/ship-caracara.md`): hand-built like the PEREGRINE, not a Ludo hero. It is the first
  twin-boom ship: a broad wing, a short pod, and the open middle between its booms. The ship file goes to
  rev 4, `generator = "hand"`, when this draft is approved.

## REDLINE as a 3D level (direction for `src/s3.rs`)

The same method as S1 and S2: geometry rendered in strips under the stage light with cast shadows, ground
in void and dark only, `light` for paint and glints, `hot` for crystal.
- **The hard low sun** is the ladder's S3 `light`, from the upper left, low, with almost no fill
  (ambient 0.08), approved together with this draft.
  - Everything that stands up casts a long shadow to the lower right, and the shadows are solid red rock
    (void).
  - The sand is orange (dark) where the sun reaches it.
  - The stage is drawn in shadows: telegraph poles, sleepers, trestle beams, boxcars, water-tower legs.
- **OPEN DESERT** (screens 0-9):
  - Dunes as a height field with sharp crests; each lee side falls into void.
  - Flat rock outcrops with void sides.
  - A lone two-lane highway: void asphalt, faded centre dashes in light checker, sand drifting over its
    edges, a rusted signpost.
  - One railway straight up the frame: dark ballast, void sleepers, rails glinting.
  - Telegraph poles whose thin shadows cross the sand, cyan crystal clusters in the dunes.
- **MESA CANYON** (screens 9-17): the deepest shadows in the game.
  - The canyon floor sits in void, with the sun only on the far floor.
  - Mesa tops are dark with void scrub, and the walls drop sheer.
  - Hot crystal veins run down the walls.
  - The twin track weaves along the floor and passes under rock through square tunnel mouths, with
    rockfall boulders, fallen poles and a rusted signal gantry.
  - The SIDEWINDER's straight run is the left third of the floor.
- **TRESTLE** (set piece, screens 16-17): the stage's showcase for the shadow map.
  - A wide dry riverbed of cracked mud (dark, with void cracks) and round stones.
  - A tall timber-and-steel trestle carries the line across it. The low sun throws its lattice of beams
    across the mud as a long criss-cross shadow.
- **FREIGHT YARD** (screens 17-26):
  - A fan of a dozen tracks with switch points and buffer stops.
  - Rows of boxcars and flatcars: box geometry with ridged roofs and long shadows.
  - Water towers on splayed legs, a turntable pit, a corrugated engine shed.
  - Conical ore heaps in hot beside the tracks.
  - At the end, the tracks converge under the crane's footprint: the TRESTLE TITAN's rails.
- **The animation loop** (`ANIM_FRAMES` copies, everything keyed to world position):
  - sand streaming off the dune crests;
  - tumbleweeds rolling across the highway;
  - glints running along the rails;
  - sand pouring from the canyon rim into the shadow;
  - the signal gantry's lamps changing;
  - an ore conveyor feeding a car in the yard;
  - the crystal pulsing up the veins and ore heaps and creeping, as S1's does.

  Not in the loop: a turning turntable (a 2.1 s loop is too short for it) and heat haze (four colors
  cannot shimmer).
- **Budget:** S3 gets its own REU image (the per-level load).
