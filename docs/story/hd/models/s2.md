# S2 models

The S2 roster in HD, and the direction for TERRACES' 3D level.

## Notes per entity

- **PIKE**: a needle that runs the vine rows; it yaws to its rail and never banks.
- **KESTREL**: the first enemy *aircraft* shaped like the player's: a captured Accord jet. It stays dark
  so the cream PEREGRINE is the only light ship on screen.
- **ROOF HATCH, BELL TURRET, DOME MORTAR**: rooftop and ground units are sprites over the panorama, as
  in S1. Each one replaces the roof or dome the level draws at its spot, with the same tiles and the same
  light, so it sits *in* the village, not on it. Telegraphs are armed frames (hot muzzles), never blinks.
- **WASP, PILLBOX**: S1's models, recolored by the palette.
- **CAMPANILE**: the tower is level geometry. The crown and the bell hub are the fight: 8 spin samples,
  because the crown has 8 points.
- **ASTROLABE**: the dome is the body. Its three rings turn independently over it, 16 samples each over
  their 6-fold symmetry, which makes every attack a combination lock you can read.

## TERRACES as a 3D level (direction for `src/s2.rs`)

The same method as S1 (`s1.rs`): geometry rendered in strips under the stage light with cast shadows,
ground in void and dark only, `light` for paint and highlights, `hot` for crystal.
- **The afternoon sun** comes from the upper right, warmer and lower than S1's morning. Terraces step down
  into their own shade, and cypress shadows fall long to the lower left. This is the ladder's S2 `light`,
  approved together with this draft.
- **VINEYARDS** (screens 0-9): contour terraces with dry-stone retaining walls whose lit tops show `dark`
  over void steps.
  - Vine rows: long parallel ridges following the contours. The rows PIKEs run on are the level's own
    lines.
  - Cypress lines as tall cones with long shadows.
  - Farmhouses with hipped tile roofs; a well; a zig-zag dirt track.
- **AQUEDUCT** (set piece, screen 8): a deep ravine in void with a thin `light` stream at its floor.
  - A stone aqueduct of tall arches crosses it. The arches' shadows fall into the ravine, and its water
    channel catches a `light` glint.
  - Crystal grows along one span.
- **HILL VILLAGE** (screens 9-17): roofs of every size and pitch, so each catches the sun differently.
  - Courtyards, a fountain plaza of pale stone circles, striped market awnings, laundry lines.
  - The bell tower (the CAMPANILE's body) in the central square.
  - Crystal cracking through the paving toward it.
- **OBSERVATORY** (screens 17-26): domes of different sizes, with raked gravel paths in curves between
  them.
  - Hedges, a long instrument hall with a copper roof, a sundial court whose gnomon casts the sun's shadow.
  - The great dome (the ASTROLABE) at the end.
- **The animation loop** (`ANIM_FRAMES` copies, everything keyed to world position):
  - cypresses swaying in a slow breeze, their shadows with them;
  - vine leaves shimmering in rows, as a light checker riding down them;
  - the aqueduct's water glinting along the channel and the stream;
  - laundry and awnings stirring;
  - the observatory domes' small shutters creeping;
  - the fountain's ripples;
  - the crystal pulsing up the cracks and creeping, as S1's does.
- **Budget:** S2 gets its own REU image (the per-level load). S1's image is full at 98.9 %.
