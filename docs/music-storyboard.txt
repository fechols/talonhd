TALON - MUSIC STORYBOARD AND SUNO PROMPTS
=========================================

How to use this file with Suno (method from pummel's docs/suno-prompting-notes.md)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Custom mode. Instrumental toggle ON for every cue.
- Paste the STYLES block into "Styles" (each is under 1,000 characters) and the LYRICS block into
  "Lyrics". The first lyrics line is always [Instrumental]; the rest are structure tags only.
- Paste the EXCLUDE line into "Exclude Styles" (Advanced).
- Style Influence high / Variety 0; Weirdness 30-40%. Max Mode for anything over two minutes.
- Generate 2-4 takes and keep the best.
  LOOP cues: pick the steadiest cycle, cut on a bar boundary at zero crossings with a 5-20 ms
  crossfade, export WAV.
  ONE-SHOT cues: Suno tends to overshoot jingles; Crop to the phrase.
- Delivery for the C64 Ultimate: 16 kHz mono DLT5 after encoding. Keep mixes punchy with a clear bass
  line and sparse high end; the sampler path has no room for washy reverb tails.
- File naming for drop-in: assets/music/<cue id>.wav (e.g. assets/music/cue.stage_s1.wav). Keep the
  original Suno download too; it is the byte-exact source.

The TALON sound (base style, reused in every cue so the soundtrack hangs together)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mid-1980s arcade shooter soundtrack played as energetic synth-rock: FM-synth brass and bell leads,
driving analog synth bass, gated-reverb drums, rock guitar power chords, bright punchy mix, constant
tempo. Heroic and bright, never grim. Every theme carries the "talon call": a rising three-note brass
motif (root, fifth, octave), so the player hears the ship in every stage.
